Noto Sans CJK SC Regular, Sans 2.004, by the Noto project.
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see OFL.txt.

Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/blob/523d033d6cb47f4a80c58a35753646f5c3608a78/Sans/OTF/SimplifiedChinese/NotoSansCJKsc-Regular.otf

SHA-256: 2c76254f6fc379fddfce0a7e84fb5385bb135d3e399294f6eeb6680d0365b74b

The unmodified font is bundled (about 16 MB) to make English and Chinese rendering
deterministic without system fonts or a first-run font download. Unsupported
characters, including some emoji, are replaced with `?` rather than rendering
invisible glyphs. Game art belongs to its respective owners and is not covered
by the font license.
